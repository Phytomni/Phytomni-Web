This Privacy Policy explains how CAAS BRI collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use Phytomni (website, accounts, chat/API, cloud storage, and related features). It should be read together with the Terms of Service.

## 1. Introduction and controller

CAAS BRI is the personal-information processor / controller for Phytomni. This Policy covers Phytomni’s public site, account registration and login, conversational and agent features, cloud storage, asynchronous analysis, and related support and security operations.

If you have questions about personal information, contact bri-zhbgs@caas.cn.

## 2. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

- **Account data:** email address, hashed password, and optional organization or title information if you provide it
- **Usage and logs:** IP address, device and browser type, timestamps, and security or operation audit records needed to operate and protect the service (described in plain language; we do not expose internal table names)
- **User content:** prompts, uploads, gene- or experiment-related inputs, and cloud-stored objects. We do not automatically label research data as sensitive; you are responsible for sensitivity classification and for having authority to upload
- **Cookies and local storage:** session tokens, language preference, and similar client-side settings needed for login state and locale

We collect information you submit directly, information generated by your use of the service, and limited technical data from your browser or client.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve Phytomni features you request
- Authenticate users, prevent abuse, and protect security
- Provide support and respond to requests
- Improve models and products by default (see Section 4)
- Comply with legal obligations and maintain necessary audits
- Send service-related notices (for example, security or material policy updates)

We do not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with this Policy without a further legal basis or notice where required.

## 4. Improvement and opt-out

By default, we may use inputs and related data to improve models and services. You may opt out by emailing bri-zhbgs@caas.cn. After opt-out, new data from your account will not be used for improvement. Model parameters already trained are not rolled back.

Opt-out does not stop processing required to run Phytomni, secure accounts, prevent abuse, provide support, or meet legal duties. An in-product opt-out control may be added in a later release; until then, email is the supported channel.

## 5. Legal basis

Depending on your location and the processing activity, we rely on one or more of the following:

- **Contract:** processing needed to provide the account and features you request
- **Legitimate interests:** security, abuse prevention, service improvement (subject to opt-out), and product analytics that do not override your rights
- **Consent:** where required (for example, certain optional disclosures or marketing, if ever offered)
- **Legal obligation:** compliance with applicable law, lawful requests, and mandatory retention

For users in mainland China, we process personal information under the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) and related rules, including notice-and-consent and other statutory bases where applicable.

## 6. Sharing and processors

We share personal information only as needed to operate Phytomni, including with processors that host or process data on our behalf. Processors may include:

- Huawei Cloud Object Storage Service (OBS)
- Huawei Cloud EIHealth (asynchronous analysis)
- SMTP email delivery providers
- Sentry or similar error-monitoring tools, if enabled

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with institutional reorganization to the extent permitted by law. Processors are instructed to process data only for authorized purposes.

## 7. International transfers

Personal information may be processed via infrastructure inside or outside mainland China, depending on operational configuration (for example, cloud storage, compute, email, or monitoring paths). Exact region lists and transfer safeguards are an operations checklist item before final publish and remain subject to institute confirmation.

Where cross-border transfers occur, we will apply contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards required by applicable law. Draft status: detailed transfer table to be completed by ops before removing the draft watermark.

## 8. Retention

We retain account data for the life of the account and for additional periods required by law or legitimate security and audit needs. Conversations and files that you can delete in-product are removed from active systems within a reasonable period after deletion, subject to backup and audit-log windows.

Backup copies and security logs may persist for a limited time after deletion or account closure, then are purged or anonymized according to retention schedules. Retention periods may be adjusted for legal holds or dispute resolution.

## 9. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of Phytomni, including TLS in transit, access controls, password hashing, rate limiting, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong unique password and protect your credentials.

If we become aware of a personal-information security incident that requires notification under applicable law, we will notify affected users and/or authorities as required.

## 10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Access and obtain a copy of your personal information
- Correct inaccurate personal information
- Delete personal information or close your account
- Export portable copies of certain data you provided
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Opt out of improvement uses (Section 4)
- Lodge a complaint with a competent authority

**PIPL (mainland China):** you may exercise rights of access, correction, deletion, and related PIPL rights by contacting us.  
**GDPR (EEA/UK):** where GDPR applies, you may also object to certain processing and request restriction, subject to legal limits.  
**CCPA/CPRA (California):** we do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are commonly defined. You may request know/delete/correct rights as applicable.

To exercise rights, email bri-zhbgs@caas.cn. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Some rights may be limited where we must retain data for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

## 11. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.
